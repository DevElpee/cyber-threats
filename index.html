Know the Threats

Understanding the risk is the first step toward prevention.

🎣

Phishing

Fake messages or websites designed to trick users into revealing information or clicking unsafe links.

🦠

Malware

Harmful software that can disrupt devices, steal information, or gain unauthorized access.

🔐

Weak Passwords

Simple or reused passwords can make accounts easier for attackers to compromise.

🚨

Unauthorized Access

Attackers may attempt to enter accounts or systems without permission.

Protect Yourself

Simple habits can significantly reduce everyday cyber risk.

  • Use strong, unique passwords for important accounts.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication whenever it is available.
  • Check links and senders carefully before clicking or replying.
  • Keep your operating system, apps, and security tools updated.
  • Never share passwords or sensitive information through suspicious messages.
  • Back up important files and report suspicious activity promptly.
